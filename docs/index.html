Photo-to-text reader

Your browser reads the photo on your device. Choose handwritten or printed text. Handwritten notes are split into image lines and read one line at a time. The first handwriting scan downloads the reader and may take a few minutes. Compare the transcript with the highlighted image before filling the form.

For a difficult line, drag a box around that line on the photo, then choose Read selected line. Its text will be added to your transcript.
